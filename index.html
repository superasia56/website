A Message for Respected Sir HASSAN ALI TOOR📜❤️

Sir, I honestly don’t think I have ever come across a teacher like you, and I don’t think I ever will.

One of the things I admire most about you is your patience. No matter how much noise there is in class, you never lose your temper. You always speak with such calmness, politeness, and kindness. The patience with which you explain things makes me listen carefully and genuinely understand what you are teaching.

I also truly admire the way you answer our questions. You never simply give an answer and move on—you explain things in a way that makes the concept stay in my mind. You go deep into every topic, give examples, and explain things from different perspectives until everything becomes clear. I genuinely appreciate the way you teach. 👏🏻

And Sir, how could I possibly forget your roasting? 😂 You are literally the King of roasting.🙉Sometimes you say something so unexpectedly savage that the only thing left for the person is to sit there and accept their fate. 😭😂 Sometimes i genuinely wonder-did you do a diploma in roasting,or do u actually have a degree in it?🤔Because the way you roast your students,it definitely feels professionally trained!😭😅 The speed at which you come up with these comebacks is honestly impressive. At this point, whenever someone says something in class, there is always a tiny fear that Sir might turn it into a roast. 😂

And offcourse,your iconic line, i dont know how but whenever you say it,it somehow makes the wholeclass conversation even funnier,and now it has become one of those things that instantly reminds us of you.Honestly,with all your roasting and those random funny moments,there is never a dull moment around you.Somehow,you always manage the whole atmosphere entertaining.😁👍

You probably don’t even realize it Sir, but you have changed many things in me simply through your lectures. There is so much to learn from the person you are, from your thoughts, your wisdom, and the way you guide and teach. There is so much I have learned simply by listening to you and observing the way you teach and guide us. Your lectures have taught me much more than what is written in the course. The things you tell us beyond studies, the guidance you give us, and also the things you teach us about Islam have given me so much to learn, reflect on, and carry with me.And i believe that is one of the greatest signs of a truly great teacher-that their lessons dont just remain in the classroom;they leave a meaningful impact on students.

You are truly one in a million, Sir. I deeply appreciate your dedication, your patience, your kindness, and the way you make learning feel meaningful. A teacher like you is not simply someone who teaches a subject—you are someone from whom there is always something valuable to learn.😇

So, thank you, Sir.❤️ Thank you for your patience, your kindness, your teaching, your guidance, and for being the kind of teacher whose words stay with me even after the lecture ends.

I may not always say it, but you are genuinely MY MOST FAVOURITE TEACHER EVER❤️🫠. And honestly, I feel incredibly lucky to have been given the opportunity to learn from a teacher like you. Having the chance to be taught by someone I respect and admire so deeply is something I will always be grateful for, and I will always cherish the lessons I have learned from you.

With Respect & Gratitude,

From Your Student, Asia🌍️
